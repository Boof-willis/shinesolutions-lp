How much does window cleaning cost?

It depends on the number of windows, stories, inside/outside, and how much hard-water buildup there is. The estimate is free; fill out the form and we’ll give you an exact price.

Can you get rid of the white spots from my sprinklers?

Those spots are mineral deposits from hard water. We treat them and rinse with purified, mineral-free water so the glass dries clear. Heavy, years-old buildup can etch glass; we’ll tell you honestly what to expect when we see it.

Are screens and tracks included?

Yes. Screens, tracks, sills and frames are part of every clean.

What if I’m not happy with a window?

Tell us and we’ll come back and re-clean it. That’s our streak-free guarantee.

Do I need to be home?

For inside windows, yes. For exterior-only cleans, usually not; we’ll confirm when we schedule.

How often should I get my windows cleaned?

It depends on your sprinklers, the dust, and how much the spots bother you. If you’d like a regular schedule, just ask when you book.
